Lawrence's PCB Projects

Three PCB boards I designed in Altium, had fabricated, and brought up on the bench as part of UTAT Space Systems. Open the schematic, layout, 3D model, and BOM below. Visualization is done through Altium Viewer so there's nothing to install. Sources are on my GitHub.

STM32L496RG breakout

Bring-up notes and scope captures on GitHub

An STM32L496RG breakout board, with buffered I²C bus, CAN through a protected transceiver, and SWD with SWO trace. Timer/PWM, NRST, and SPI pins are also broken out to a 7-pin header. Verified on the scope after assembly.
